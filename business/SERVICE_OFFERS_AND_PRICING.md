# Productized Service Offers & Pricing Sheet

> Fixed-scope packages are far easier to sell than hourly work — the client knows
> the price and outcome upfront, and you protect your margin with clear boundaries.
>
> **Pricing note:** Numbers below are illustrative *placeholders* shown in both
> USD and INR ranges. Set your real prices based on your local market, your day
> rate, and the value delivered. A good starting point: know your **minimum day
> rate**, estimate effort in days, then add a margin and round to a clean package
> price. Currency symbol: adjust to your market.

---

## Pricing Principles

1. **Charge for outcomes, not hours.** "A working clinic system in 30 days," not "160 hours."
2. **Always take a deposit** — 50% upfront, 50% on delivery (or milestone-based for big projects).
3. **Anchor with three tiers.** Most clients pick the middle one.
4. **Recurring revenue is the goal.** Every project should lead into a support/hosting retainer.
5. **Scope creep = change order.** Anything outside the SOW is a new, separately-priced item.

## Payment Terms (state these on every quote)

- **Deposit:** 50% to start (or 30% deposit + milestone payments on larger projects).
- **Final payment:** Due on delivery / before production go-live.
- **Retainers:** Billed monthly in advance.
- **Late payment:** Define a grace period and a late fee.
- **Currency & taxes:** State currency and that prices exclude applicable taxes (GST/VAT).
- **Scope changes:** Quoted separately as change orders before work begins.

---

## How to Quote (quick method)

1. Estimate the effort in **working days**.
2. Multiply by your **minimum day rate** (the floor you'll never go below).
3. Add **20–40% margin** for risk, revisions, and project management.
4. Round to a clean package number.
5. Present **three options** (good / better / best) so the client chooses *how much*, not *whether*.
