# Contract & SOW Templates

> ⚠️ **Important:** These are practical, plain-language starting templates — **not
> legal advice.** Laws differ by country. Before you rely on these for real
> clients, have a local lawyer review them once and adapt them to your
> jurisdiction (especially IP, liability, healthcare/data-privacy, and tax clauses).
> After that one review you can reuse them for years.

Replace all **[BRACKETED]** fields. Three documents are included:
1. **Mutual NDA** — for early conversations
2. **Master Services Agreement (MSA)** — the umbrella terms, signed once per client
3. **Statement of Work (SOW)** — per-project scope and price, references the MSA

---

# 1. Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement** ("Agreement") is entered into on
**[DATE]** between:

- **[COMPANY]**, [address] ("Company"), and
- **[CLIENT]**, [address] ("Client").

Collectively the "Parties."

**1. Purpose.** The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship and
may share confidential information.

**2. Confidential Information.** Any non-public business, technical, or financial
information disclosed by one Party to the other, whether written or oral, marked
confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

**3. Obligations.** Each Party will: (a) keep the other's Confidential Information
secret; (b) use it only for the Purpose; (c) protect it with at least reasonable
care; and (d) not disclose it to third parties without prior written consent.

**4. Exclusions.** Information that is public, already known, independently
developed, or rightfully received from a third party is not confidential.

**5. Term.** This Agreement lasts **[2 years]** from the date above;
confidentiality obligations survive for **[3 years]** after disclosure.

**6. Return.** On request, each Party will return or destroy the other's
Confidential Information.

**7. No License.** Nothing here grants any IP rights.

**8. Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of **[JURISDICTION]**.

Signed:

`____________________`  [COMPANY]   Date: ________
`____________________`  [CLIENT]    Date: ________

# 2. Master Services Agreement (MSA)

**This Master Services Agreement** ("Agreement") is made on **[DATE]** between
**[COMPANY]** ("Provider") and **[CLIENT]** ("Client").

### 1. Services
Provider will perform software development and consulting services as described
in one or more **Statements of Work (SOWs)** that reference this Agreement. Each
SOW is incorporated into this Agreement.

### 2. Statements of Work
Each SOW will specify scope, deliverables, timeline, fees, and payment schedule.
If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, the SOW controls for that project.

### 3. Fees & Payment
- Fees are stated in each SOW. Unless stated otherwise: **50% deposit** to begin,
  remainder due on delivery.
- Invoices are payable within **[14] days**.
- Late amounts accrue interest of **[1.5%] per month** (or the legal maximum).
- Fees exclude taxes (GST/VAT); Client is responsible for applicable taxes.
- Reasonable pre-approved expenses are reimbursable.

### 4. Change Requests
Any change to an SOW's scope must be agreed in writing as a **Change Order** with
its own price and timeline impact before work proceeds.

### 5. Client Responsibilities
Client will provide timely access, information, approvals, and a designated point
of contact. Delays caused by Client may shift timelines and costs.

### 6. Intellectual Property
- Upon **full payment** for a deliverable, Provider assigns to Client all IP
  rights in the **custom work product** created specifically for Client under the
  relevant SOW.
- Provider retains ownership of its **pre-existing materials, tools, libraries,
  and know-how** ("Provider Tools") and grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive
  license to use Provider Tools as embedded in the deliverables.
- Third-party and open-source components remain under their own licenses.

### 7. Confidentiality
Each Party will protect the other's confidential information per the same terms
as the NDA above (or the separately signed NDA, which survives).

### 8. Warranty
Provider warrants that services will be performed in a professional, workmanlike
manner. For **[30] days** after delivery, Provider will fix defects in the
deliverables at no charge. **EXCEPT AS STATED, SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS"
WITHOUT OTHER WARRANTIES.**

### 9. Limitation of Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party is liable for indirect,
incidental, or consequential damages. **Provider's total liability under this
Agreement will not exceed the fees paid for the SOW giving rise to the claim.**

### 10. Term & Termination
- This Agreement continues until terminated. Either Party may terminate with
  **[30] days** written notice (or immediately for material breach uncured after
  **[14] days** notice).
- On termination, Client pays for work performed up to the termination date.

### 11. Independent Contractor
Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent of Client.

### 12. Non-Solicitation
During the engagement and for **[12] months** after, neither Party will solicit
the other's employees/contractors involved in the work without consent.

### 13. General
This Agreement (with its SOWs) is the entire agreement; amendments must be in
writing; it is governed by the laws of **[JURISDICTION]**; disputes will be
resolved in the courts of **[VENUE]** (or by arbitration, if preferred).

Signed:

`____________________`  [COMPANY]   Date: ________
`____________________`  [CLIENT]    Date: ________

## Usage Checklist

- [ ] Have a local lawyer review all three once and adapt to **[JURISDICTION]**.
- [ ] For **healthcare clients**, add data-privacy/compliance clauses (patient data,
      retention, breach notification) appropriate to your region.
- [ ] Sign **NDA** early, **MSA** once per client, a new **SOW** per project.
- [ ] Never start work without a signed SOW **and** the deposit received.
- [ ] Keep signed copies organized (you can even store them in Odoo).
